What you get in every kit

  • Music – ~50 instrumental tracks shipped as A and B variants, so cues rotate without the player noticing repetition.
  • Ambience beds – long-form room tone for every space in the setting.
  • 25 seamless loops – real places, not drones: fireplace, cave drips, neon rain, saloon interior, sunken city. Every loop file has "Loop" in its name.
  • 120 SFX – the gameplay set the genre actually needs, from Sword Unsheathe to Hack Success to Revolver Cock.
  • Format – 48 kHz stereo, peak-limited to -0.3 dBFS, folders by category, filenames that say what the sound is: DarkDungeonAudioKit_SFX_IronDoorSlam, never sfx_047.
  • Licence – royalty-free for unlimited commercial and non-commercial projects, no attribution. You may not resell or redistribute the files themselves as a sound library.
How it's made. All audio is produced with AI audio generation tools (Suno, Stable Audio Open, Kling Sound), then curated from a much larger generated set, peak-normalised, converted and named by hand. Every store page is tagged AI-generated, so you can filter it out if that is not for you.
